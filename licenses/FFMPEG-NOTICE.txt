FrameCover bundles unmodified FFmpeg executables (ffmpeg, ffprobe).

FFmpeg is a separate program, run as a child process. The bundled builds
are configured with --enable-gpl --enable-version3 (they include libx264,
libaom and other GPL components) and are therefore distributed under the
GNU General Public License, version 3 -- see FFmpeg-GPL-3.0.txt.
FrameCover itself is MIT-licensed (see LICENSE).

Builds and their sources:
  Windows, Linux:  FFmpeg n8.1.3, built by BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
                   https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
                   (build scripts and per-release source references are
                   published there; the exact archive URLs and SHA-256
                   checksums are listed in xtask/ffmpeg-pins.toml)
  macOS:           FFmpeg 8.1.2, built by Martin Riedl
                   https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de
FFmpeg source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
                    https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git

If you received FrameCover in binary form and cannot obtain the corresponding
FFmpeg source from the links above, open an issue at
https://github.com/fgagarin/framecover/issues and the source will be provided
for at least three years from the release date.
