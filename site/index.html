01

Precise frame selection

Scrub the timeline and preview your chosen frame before applying a new thumbnail.

02

Popular formats

Open MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI and FLV files. How the thumbnail is saved depends on the container.

03

Convert to MP4

For other containers, the app offers to create a separate MP4. Your original file stays untouched.

04

Local processing

Your video is processed on your computer. No server uploads or account required.

05

Safe saving

Changes are written to a temporary file before replacing the original, so a failed write won't leave your source video corrupted.

06

Desktop and phone

The same straightforward workflow on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. Ready-to-use builds are published on GitHub.

Not every container supports embedded cover art. FLV and WebM use an XDG cache thumbnail where supported; results vary across operating systems and players.
